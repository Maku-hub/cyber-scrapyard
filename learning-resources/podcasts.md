# Podcasts

Audio to keep up with the field and absorb war stories on the commute — from
narrative deep-dives to weekly news roundups. All free.

## Storytelling & deep dives

- [Darknet Diaries](https://darknetdiaries.com) — beautifully produced true
  stories from the dark side of the internet: breaches, hackers, and social
  engineering. The best on-ramp for newcomers.
- [Malicious Life](https://malicious.life) — the history of cybersecurity told
  as narrative episodes; great context on how today's threats came to be.
- [Hacking Humans](https://thecyberwire.com/podcasts/hacking-humans) — social
  engineering, phishing, and scams broken down each week.

## News & industry

- [Risky Business](https://risky.biz) — sharp weekly security news and analysis
  with practitioner interviews; a staple for staying current.
- [Security Now](https://twit.tv/shows/security-now) — long-running deep technical
  weekly on vulnerabilities, crypto, and privacy.
- [SANS Internet Storm Center — StormCast](https://isc.sans.edu/podcast.html) —
  a short daily brief on emerging threats and notable activity.
- [CyberWire Daily](https://thecyberwire.com/podcasts/daily-podcast) — concise
  daily cybersecurity news.
