# Blue Team Platforms

Hands-on platforms for defensive skills — SOC analysis, incident response,
threat hunting, digital forensics, and detection engineering. The blue-team
counterpart to the offensive practice sites in
[Platforms & Creators](platforms-and-creators.md).

## Practice platforms

- [CyberDefenders](https://cyberdefenders.org) — free, high-quality blue-team
  challenges and labs (PCAP analysis, DFIR, malware, SIEM) built around real
  artifacts. Excellent for practicing investigations end to end.
- [LetsDefend](https://letsdefend.io) — a simulated SOC environment with a
  realistic alert queue and guided investigations; strong free tier for
  learning the analyst workflow.
- [Blue Team Labs Online](https://blueteamlabs.online) — investigation-style
  challenges and labs across security analysis, IR, and forensics, with both
  free and premium content.

## Notes

- These pair naturally with the tooling in the forensics, log-analysis, and
  detection sections — bring your own [CyberChef](../dev-tools/cyberchef.md) and
  [jq](../dev-tools/jq.md) for parsing artifacts.
- Great complements to certification study (Security+, blue-team tracks) in
  [Certifications](certifications.md).
