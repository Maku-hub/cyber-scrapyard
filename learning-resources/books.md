# Books

A personal selection of books that give a solid, practical foundation across
networking, Linux, programming, and ethical hacking. Not the only path — but a
dependable one for beginners through intermediate learners.

## Networking

- **Mike Meyers, Scott Jernigan — *CompTIA Network+ All-in-One Exam Guide***
  Beginner-friendly, broad coverage of networking fundamentals. A great warm-up
  before security topics.
- **Adam Józefiok — *CCNA 200-120*** *(Polish)*
  Clear introduction to networking basics, routing, switching, and Cisco
  concepts. *(Targets the retired 200-120 exam — the current CCNA is 200-301 —
  but the fundamentals still hold up as a primer.)*

## Linux

- **Christopher Negus — *Linux Bible***
  One of the most complete Linux references — commands, system administration,
  shells, and common workflows. Ideal for beginners to intermediate users.
- **Karol Szafrański — *Twierdza Linux*** *(Polish)*
  Practical, security-focused Linux book — great for understanding hardening,
  permissions, and real defensive practices.

## Programming & exploitation fundamentals

- **Justin Seitz, Tim Arnold — *Black Hat Python***
  How Python is used in offensive security: writing tools, exploits, and
  backdoors. Very hands-on.
- **Jon Erickson — *Hacking: The Art of Exploitation***
  A classic. Teaches low-level topics (memory, assembly, exploitation) through
  practical examples. More advanced, but very rewarding.

## Cybersecurity

- **Georgia Weidman — *Penetration Testing: A Hands-On Introduction to Hacking***
  One of the best beginner pentest books — tools, methodology, and real
  techniques.
- **Peter Kim — *The Hacker Playbook 2 & 3***
  Practical guides full of realistic attack scenarios and workflows. A good
  bridge from beginner to intermediate.
- **Arthur Conklin, Greg White — *CompTIA Security+ All-in-One Exam Guide***
  Solid security theory — risk, cryptography, policies, architecture. Good for
  organizing foundational knowledge.
- **Securitum — *Wprowadzenie do bezpieczeństwa IT, Vol. 1 & 2*** *(Polish)*
  Modern, clear introduction to key security concepts, attacks, and defenses.
