# News & Feeds

Frameworks, advisories, and sources to follow so you understand attacker
behavior, track vulnerabilities as they land, and keep up with what's happening.
Read the frameworks once and revisit them; skim the news feeds daily.

## Frameworks & standards

- **MITRE ATT&CK** — https://attack.mitre.org
  The reference knowledge base of adversary tactics and techniques (TTPs).
  Everyone maps detections, red-team plans, and threat reports to it.
- **OWASP Top Ten** — https://owasp.org/www-project-top-ten/
  The canonical list of the most critical web application security risks —
  the starting point for web app security.
- **CIS Benchmarks** — https://www.cisecurity.org/cis-benchmarks
  Consensus hardening guides for operating systems, cloud, and software. The
  practical baseline when you need to *secure* a system, not just attack it.

## Vulnerability data

- **NVD (NIST National Vulnerability Database)** — https://nvd.nist.gov
  Authoritative CVE data enriched with CVSS scores and affected-product info.
- **CVE Details** — https://www.cvedetails.com
  Friendly front end for browsing CVEs by vendor, product, and version.
- **CVE.org** — https://www.cve.org
  The MITRE-run source of record for CVE identifiers.
- **CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV)** — https://www.cisa.gov/known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog
  The short list of CVEs *actively exploited in the wild* — patch these first.
- **Exploit-DB** — https://www.exploit-db.com
  Archive of public exploits and PoCs, cross-referenced to CVEs.

## Phishing & malware feeds

- **OpenPhish** — https://openphish.com
  Live feed of confirmed phishing URLs.
- **PhishTank** — https://phishtank.org
  Community-verified phishing URL database (with a free API).
- **URLhaus (abuse.ch)** — https://urlhaus.abuse.ch
  Feed of URLs distributing malware.
- **MalwareBazaar (abuse.ch)** — https://bazaar.abuse.ch
  Searchable repository of malware samples and hashes for analysis.

## News

- **Hacker News** — https://news.ycombinator.com
  Broad tech/security discussion; big incidents and disclosures surface here fast.
- **The Hacker News** — https://thehackernews.com
  High-volume security news site.
- **BleepingComputer** — https://www.bleepingcomputer.com
  Reliable coverage of breaches, ransomware, and vulnerabilities.
- **Krebs on Security** — https://krebsonsecurity.com
  In-depth investigative reporting on cybercrime.
- **r/netsec** — https://www.reddit.com/r/netsec/
  Curated feed of technical security research and write-ups.

## Vendor & research blogs

- **Google Project Zero** — https://googleprojectzero.blogspot.com
  Deep vulnerability research and 0-day analysis.
- **Cisco Talos** — https://blog.talosintelligence.com
  Threat intelligence, malware teardowns, and advisories.
- **Microsoft MSRC / Sysinternals** — https://msrc.microsoft.com/blog/ and
  https://learn.microsoft.com/sysinternals/
  Windows security response and the essential Sysinternals tooling.
- **Sekurak** — https://sekurak.pl *(Polish)*
  Well-regarded practical security articles and tutorials.
- **CERT Polska** — https://cert.pl *(Polish/English)*
  National CERT advisories and malware/botnet analyses.
