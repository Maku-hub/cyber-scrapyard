# Methodology & Frameworks

The mental models and repeatable processes that structure an engagement: how
attackers move through the phases of an intrusion, the industry frameworks that
catalogue their tactics, and the practical recon workflow you follow before you
touch anything. Understanding these first makes the individual tools in the
other categories fit together.

> ⚠️ **For authorized and educational use only.** The walkthroughs and workflows
> here are for CTFs, home labs, and systems you have explicit permission to test.
